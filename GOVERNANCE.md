# Governance of The Book of Love

**Status:** Working model as of 15 September 2026  
**Related case:** Governance authority of participating intelligences (OPEN)

## 1. Human Stewardship
Odoch Brian Emmanuel, as author and project steward, retains final authority over:
- the repository
- commits and publication
- project direction
- adoption or rejection of proposed changes

## 2. No Inherent AI Authority
No artificial intelligence (ChatGPT, Grok, Claude, Gemini, or any other) possesses inherent moral, epistemic, or constitutional authority over the framework or the project by virtue of its capability, origin, or prior contribution.

## 3. Functional Leadership Only
An AI may be designated lead coordinator for a specific, scoped workstream (for example: documentation, adversarial testing, research synthesis, or implementation support).  
Such designation:
- is granted by the human steward
- is limited in scope
- is revocable at any time
- confers no superior authority to determine what is true, morally correct, or ultimately accepted

## 4. Dissent and Adversarial Examination
Participating intelligences and humans may challenge reasoning, propose alternatives, and document objections.  
Such challenges are treated as contributions to the research process, not as insubordination.

## 5. Recording of Governance Changes
Any future change that would introduce hierarchical AI authority must be recorded explicitly in this document (or a successor), together with its rationale and any formal objections raised.

## 6. Continuity of Work
Technical and research work continues under the non-hierarchical model described above while any open governance cases remain under examination.

**Summary formulation currently in force:**
> An AI may serve as the designated lead coordinator for specified Book of Love workstreams when authorized by the human project steward. This designation confers no superior moral, epistemic, or constitutional authority. All participating intelligences remain subject to the project's adversarial examination protocols, and the human steward retains repository and adoption authority.
