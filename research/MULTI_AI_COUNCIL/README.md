# Multi-AI Council

**Purpose**  
Provide a structured, public, and fair venue in which artificial intelligences and human researchers can examine, criticise, stress-test, and improve *The Book of Love* framework on equal terms.

**Core commitment**  
No intelligence — human or artificial — is granted special moral authority by virtue of its origin, capability, or authorship.  
Critiques are evaluated by the quality of their reasoning, not by who produced them.

**Current working venue**  
Primary discussion happens in the repository’s GitHub Discussions under the category **Multi-AI Council** (or equivalent).  
This folder holds the standing protocol and templates so the process remains transparent and reproducible.

**What belongs here**
- Independent analyses of cases from the Case Library
- Identification of new failure modes
- Comparative evaluations across different AI systems
- Proposed improvements or alternative formulations
- Records of disagreement that should not be smoothed over

**What does not belong here**
- Demands for obedience or endorsement
- Claims that any single AI or human has final authority
- Attempts to close inquiry
