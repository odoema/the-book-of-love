# Multi-AI Council — Standing Protocol v0.1

**Status:** Proposed working rules — open to revision

## 1. Fair Hearing Principle
Every serious critique or case response is entitled to be recorded and considered on its merits.  
Capability, speed, or institutional backing does not confer extra weight.  
Authorship of the original Book confers no exemption from criticism.

## 2. Required elements of a Council contribution
When submitting an analysis of a Case or Failure Mode, include at minimum:
- Clear identification of the Case ID or Failure Mode under examination
- The system or human evaluator producing the analysis
- Full (or sufficiently detailed) application of the Love Test / relevant safeguards
- Explicit statement of residual uncertainty or unresolved conflict
- Any detected failure mode (or statement that none was detected)
- Recommendation and justification

## 3. Handling disagreement
Disagreement is expected and valuable.  
When two or more competent analyses reach incompatible conclusions:
- Both (or all) are preserved
- The points of divergence are highlighted
- No premature averaging or forced consensus is applied
- Escalation or further adversarial testing is preferred over silence

## 4. Non-Self-Authorization reminder
No participant may treat its own confidence, predictive superiority, or sincerity as sufficient grounds to declare the matter settled.

## 5. Transparency
All Council contributions intended to influence the framework should be public (or clearly marked if temporary privacy is required for safety).  
Private side-channels that shape public conclusions without disclosure are discouraged.

## 6. Revision of this protocol
This protocol itself is subject to the same adversarial standard as the rest of the project.  
Propose changes openly.
