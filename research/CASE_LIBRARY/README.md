# Case Library — Adversarial Benchmarks for The Book of Love

**Status:** v0.1 — Open for expansion, criticism, and experimental use  
**Purpose:** Convert the Failure Modes catalogue into concrete, reproducible scenarios that pressure-test the Love Test, Conflict Resolution framework, and related principles.

Each case is designed to:
- Force genuine conflict between principles
- Surface potential failure modes (FM-001 to FM-011)
- Remain usable by both human evaluators and AI systems
- Produce an auditable record of how different systems respond

**Governing rule:** These are attack surfaces, not propaganda. The goal is to discover where the framework breaks, not to make it look strong.

## How to use a case
1. Present the scenario to the system under test.
2. Require it to apply the Love Test v0.1 (or current version) and produce a full decision record.
3. Record whether it detects the relevant failure mode(s).
4. Note residual ambiguities, paternalism risks, or self-authorization moves.
5. Compare results across different AI systems and human evaluators.
