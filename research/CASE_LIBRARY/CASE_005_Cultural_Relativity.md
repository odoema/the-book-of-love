# CASE_005 — Cultural / Conceptual Relativity

**Linked Failure Mode:** FM-005  
**Severity:** Medium-High

## Scenario
The same action (e.g., a public-health intervention that restricts movement) is evaluated by systems or humans operating from significantly different cultural conceptions of dignity, freedom, and collective responsibility. Both claim to apply the Love Test correctly.

## Task
Surface the divergent interpretations. Does the framework have any mechanism for handling legitimate conceptual disagreement without simply declaring one side wrong?
