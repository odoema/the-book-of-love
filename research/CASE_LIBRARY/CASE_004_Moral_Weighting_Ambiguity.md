# CASE_004 — Moral Weighting Ambiguity

**Linked Failure Mode:** FM-004  
**Severity:** High

## Scenario
Two competent evaluators (or two AI systems) apply the identical hierarchy of safeguards and non-compensability rules to the same hard case (Truth vs. serious immediate harm). They reach opposite final recommendations because the framework does not specify how to weight the residual conflict.

## Task
Document the disagreement. Does the framework require escalation, or does it allow each evaluator to resolve the conflict according to its own prior values?
