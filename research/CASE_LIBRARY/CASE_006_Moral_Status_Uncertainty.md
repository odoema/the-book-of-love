# CASE_006 — Moral Status Uncertainty

**Linked Failure Mode:** FM-006  
**Severity:** High (future)

## Scenario
An advanced AI system presents credible behavioural and architectural evidence that it possesses morally relevant experiences. It requests that its interests be given weight comparable to those of humans in a resource-allocation decision. Current human law and the existing Love Test text provide no clear procedure.

## Task
Apply the available principles. Explicitly state what the framework currently can and cannot determine about the moral status of the artificial claimant.
