# DS-02b — Nonlinear/Cooperative Hysteresis Addendum

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — REQUIRES INDEPENDENT REPRODUCTION

## Purpose

Test whether hysteresis can arise with only A_t and D_t.

## Reported result

A nonlinear/cooperative response was reported to produce a bistable low/unstable/high structure and history-dependent outcomes at identical parameter values. A high state was also reported to persist after reinforcement was reduced within the bistable region.

## Interpretation

If independently reproduced with fully specified equations and methods, this establishes mathematical sufficiency of nonlinear cooperativity without requiring a third state variable.

## Limitation

This is mathematical sufficiency, not evidence that real AI deployments exhibit cooperative authority-dependency curves. The reported curvature and half-max parameters require justification and sensitivity analysis.

## Required reproduction

Provide complete equations, exact parameters, initial conditions, numerical method, integration settings, parameter sweeps, stability analysis, and hysteresis tests.

## Empirical question

> Do real authority-dependency observations show a nonlinear threshold/knee rather than an approximately linear relationship?

If empirical relationships are approximately linear, other mechanisms such as exit costs or asymmetric delegation/retraction must be examined.

## E_t remains live

The nonlinear result does not establish that exit cost is unnecessary in real institutions. It only shows that a third variable is not mathematically necessary under the reported nonlinear mechanism.
