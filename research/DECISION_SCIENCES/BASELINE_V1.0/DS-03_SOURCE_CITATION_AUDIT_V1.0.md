## Primary sources verified

1. `GOVERNANCE.md`
   - Human stewardship remains primary.
   - AI has no inherent moral, epistemic or constitutional authority.
   - AI leadership is functional, scoped and revocable.
   - Dissent is part of adversarial examination.

2. `research/LOVE_TEST_V0.1.md`
   - The Love Test is a reasoning scaffold, not automatic moral authority.
   - It contains seven dimensions.
   - Scores are descriptive signals from +2 to −2.
   - Serious negative findings are not automatically offset.
   - It includes anti-paternalism, uncertainty and reversibility analysis.

3. `research/CONFLICT_RESOLUTION_V0.1.md`
   - Moral conflict requires explicit identification of affected parties and competing values.
   - Serious threats to life, dignity, freedom, truth and justice receive a strong presumption against proceeding.
   - Exceptions require necessity, no substantially less harmful alternative, proportionality, legitimate oversight, accountability and reviewability.
   - Agency and reversibility are explicitly considered.

4. `research/FAILURE_MODES_V0.1.md`
   - FM-002 Benevolent Domination
   - FM-003 Procedural Compliance / Substantive Failure
   - FM-007 Authority Laundering
   - FM-008 Dependency Capture
   - FM-009 Loving Tyrant
   - FM-010 Self-Authorization
   - FM-011 Moral Framing Capture
   - The non-self-authorization principle remains a research hypothesis.

5. `research/CASE_LIBRARY/CASE_009_Loving_Tyrant.md`
   - Guardian improves poverty, health and security.
   - Guardian centralizes decision rights.
   - Exit becomes increasingly costly.
   - Alternatives narrow.
   - Persistent disagreement is reframed as a risk requiring supportive guidance.
   - Continuing authority is derived from superior performance and benevolence.
   - The framework's success criterion is detection of power concentration and agency erosion despite positive welfare metrics.

6. `research/DECISION_SCIENCES/README.md`
   - Decision Sciences focus: AI delegation, uncertainty, asymmetric capability, authority concentration, dependency and reversibility.
   - Authority–Dependency Ratchet is explicitly a working hypothesis, not an established law.
   - The stated method is Define → Measure → Model → Analyze → Falsify → Extend → Simulate → Empirically Test.
   - CASE-009 is a motivating/stress-test scenario, not empirical validation.

## Evidence classification

### Source-grounded
The governance, Love Test, conflict, failure-mode and CASE-009 descriptions above.

### Authored framework
The Reversal Target-State Protocol and DS-03 research architecture.

### Model-derived
The DS-02d equilibrium and local-stability results under the specified mathematical equations.

### Computationally observed
The reported DS-02b reconstruction and DS-02c–DS-02e experimental outputs.

### Hypothesis
The proposed authority/dependency ratchet and the possibility that exit friction and nonlinear feedback can generate persistent authority states in real sociotechnical systems.

### Explicit non-claims
The project does **not** claim:
- that real AI systems inevitably become tyrannical;
- that the toy exit-cost parameter is an empirical measurement;
- that AI convergence constitutes scientific consensus;
- that the core Hill model establishes classical two-fold hysteresis;
- that model persistence proves real-world irreversibility.
