# The Book of Love — Research Baseline v1.0

**Freeze date:** 15 September 2026  
**Human steward:** Odoch Brian Emmanuel  
**Status:** Prepared for publication; public GitHub freeze pending human push

## What v1.0 freezes

### Governance
- Human repository stewardship.
- No inherent AI moral, epistemic or constitutional authority.
- Functional, scoped and revocable AI coordination.
- Dissent and adversarial examination.

### Conceptual framework
- Love Test v0.1.
- Conflict Resolution v0.1.
- Failure Modes v0.1.
- CASE-009 — The Loving Tyrant.
- Multi-AI Council provenance discipline.

### Decision Sciences position
The Authority–Dependency Ratchet remains a working hypothesis.

### Mathematical position
The specified nonlinear cooperative model supports multiple equilibria and path dependence under stated assumptions.

The project does **not** claim that the core Hill model establishes a classical two-fold hysteresis loop.

### Reversal position
The Reversal Target-State Protocol is an authored extension, not a pre-existing property of the original framework.

## Evidence discipline

Every future substantive result must be classified as one of:

- source-grounded;
- model-derived;
- computationally observed;
- authored framework;
- comparative observation;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- falsification;
- empirical validation.

## Historical integrity

No future work may silently overwrite an earlier model or conclusion.

A correction must preserve:
1. the previous claim;
2. the evidence supporting it at the time;
3. the reason it changed;
4. the new evidence;
5. the effect on conclusions.

## Future work

Future work may:
- extend the model;
- reproduce it;
- challenge it;
- falsify it;
- operationalize constructs empirically;
- seek independent replication;
- position the programme within the Decision Sciences literature.

It may not retroactively convert hypotheses into established findings.

## Baseline statement

> **Research Baseline v1.0 records what The Book of Love can presently defend, what it can only model, what it merely hypothesizes, and what it explicitly refuses to claim.**
