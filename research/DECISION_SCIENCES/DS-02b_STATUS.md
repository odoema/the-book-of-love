# DS-02b Status

**Current status:** REPRODUCED (QUALIFIED) FOR RECONSTRUCTED HILL-COOPERATIVE MODEL CLASS

## What has been reproduced

The supplied reproduction record reports independent numerical and analytical verification of a reconstructed two-variable Hill-function cooperative model:

- bistability;
- history dependence at identical parameters;
- persistence of the high state after reinforcement reduction;
- disappearance of bistability when cooperativity is removed;
- robustness of the decreasing-parameter transition to slower sweep rates.

## Qualification

The original archived DS-02b addendum did not preserve the exact equations, parameters, initial conditions, numerical method, or original outputs. Therefore the reconstruction cannot be certified as a literal reproduction of the historical DS-02b run.

The reproduced claim is consequently indexed to the **reconstructed Hill-cooperative model class**.

## Asymmetry finding

The reconstruction exhibits a one-sided structural transition: the high branch disappears through a fold as reinforcement decreases, while the low state remains locally stable. A symmetric two-sided parameter-forced hysteresis loop was not established for this model.

## Research boundary

This is mathematical sufficiency, not empirical validation.

It does not show that real AI deployments exhibit the mechanism, and it does not show that exit cost E_t is unnecessary in real institutions.
