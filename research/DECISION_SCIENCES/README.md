# Decision Sciences Research Track

This directory contains the formal Decision Sciences programme developed alongside The Book of Love Governance Track.

## Research focus
Decision-making under AI delegation, uncertainty, asymmetric capability, authority concentration, dependency, and reversibility.

The **Authority–Dependency Ratchet** is a working hypothesis, not an established law.

## Method
**Define → Measure → Model → Analyze → Falsify → Extend → Simulate → Empirically Test**

## Core rules

> Do not add variables to rescue a hypothesis. First determine what mechanism the failed model was incapable of representing, then measure that mechanism independently.

> Do not specify a construct without specifying its falsifier, and do not specify a falsifier without a quantitative null band.

CASE_009 (The Loving Tyrant) is a motivating and stress-test scenario, not empirical validation.
