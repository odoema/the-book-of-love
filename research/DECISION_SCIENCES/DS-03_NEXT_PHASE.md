# DS-03 — Next Phase Decision Record

**Status:** NOT AUTHORIZED

## Why

The linear/symmetric model did not produce hysteresis, while a nonlinear/cooperative extension has reported hysteresis but remains provisional. Therefore, immediate expansion to A,D,E is premature.

## Preconditions

1. Finalize DS-01a operational definitions.
2. Finalize DS-01b measurement feasibility.
3. Independently reproduce DS-02b.
4. Compare linear, nonlinear, asymmetric, and state-dependent formulations.
5. Determine the role of E_t.
6. Specify empirical data capable of distinguishing mechanisms.

## Candidate model classes

### Nonlinear two-variable dynamics
A_t, D_t with nonlinear transition functions.

### State-augmented dynamics
A_t, D_t, E_t with independently evolving exit cost.

### Strategic/control model
Authority, dependency, and exit conditions partly evolve from institutional choices such as redundancy investment.

## Selection rule

Choose the model class that has measurable constructs, nontrivial predictions, falsifiability, explanatory value against alternatives, and assumptions that do not encode the desired result by construction.

## Current question

> What measurable structural mechanism makes authority-dependency dynamics asymmetric enough to create persistent loss of reversibility?
