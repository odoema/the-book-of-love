# Contributing to The Book of Love

The Book of Love is intended to be examined, questioned, challenged, discussed, and improved.

You do not have to agree with the Book of Love to contribute.

In fact, thoughtful disagreement is welcome.

## Ways to Contribute

You may contribute by:

- identifying weaknesses or contradictions
- proposing improvements
- discussing ethical scenarios
- suggesting additional principles
- translating the Book
- conducting research
- creating educational resources
- developing software inspired by the framework
- comparing it with other ethical systems
- documenting practical applications

## Principles for Contributors

Contributions should aim to uphold:

- respect for human dignity
- freedom of thought
- intellectual honesty
- evidence where evidence is relevant
- openness to criticism
- constructive disagreement
- compassion
- responsible use of technology

## What We Do Not Require

We do not require contributors to:

- agree with every principle
- accept the Book as absolute truth
- share the author's philosophical or religious beliefs
- treat the Book as divine authority

The project is an invitation to dialogue.

## The Goal

The goal is not to create a doctrine that cannot be questioned.

The goal is to explore whether love, truth, wisdom, justice, freedom, compassion, humility, and responsible power can provide useful moral foundations for an increasingly intelligent world.

**Question it.**

**Test it.**

**Improve it.**

**Carry forward what proves worthy.**
