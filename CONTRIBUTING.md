# Contributing

The Book of Love is intended to be a living forum for ethical reflection about intelligence, humanity, and the future.

Contributions are welcome from researchers, developers, philosophers, educators, policymakers, theologians, students, artists, and the public.

- Propose clarifications.
- Identify weaknesses or contradictions.
- Contribute translations and research.
- Develop practical ways to test the principles.
- Discuss how the Covenant might apply to real AI systems.
- Propose future editions without silently rewriting the historical First Edition.

Good-faith disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Editions

The original First Edition should remain preserved. Future revisions should be clearly versioned.
